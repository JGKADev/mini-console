Submix

Off

Submix gives every source a second volume, so two places can hear a different balance. Your faders set Mix A. The knobs below set Mix B. Each output listens to one of the two.

For example: put Stream on Mix B and turn Music down there. Your viewers hear quiet music while your headphones stay loud.

Submix is offEvery output hears Mix A, your faders. Switch on Power to use Mix B.
Link: Mix B follows its fader and keeps the same balance. Click to set Mix B on its own. Where the Mix A level is

Routing

Which sources reach which outputs. Each pad sets one stereo pair on the Mini's routing matrix, and lit pads pulse with their source's sound. With submix on, the top row picks whether an output hears Mix A or Mix B.
